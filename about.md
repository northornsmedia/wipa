# WIPA — Women in Intellectual Property Association

> **A Global, Empowering Community to Grow Your Network, Leadership, Career, Patents & Knowledge.**

---

## 🌍 What is WIPA?

WIPA (Women in Intellectual Property Association) is the **premier digital platform** built exclusively to advance, connect, and celebrate women professionals in the world of **Patents, Trademarks, Copyright, and Innovation**.

We are not just another networking site. WIPA is a movement. It is a thriving global ecosystem where women in IP — whether they are patent attorneys, trademark examiners, startup founders, tech innovators, or law students — come together to share knowledge, find mentors, discover career opportunities, and build lifelong professional relationships.

---

## 💡 Why Does WIPA Exist?

Women make up a significant and growing portion of the legal and technology workforce. Yet, they remain **severely underrepresented** in:

- Patent filings and prosecution
- IP partnership roles at major law firms
- Startup founder positions in deep tech
- Leadership positions in IP organizations and standards bodies

WIPA was founded by a group of passionate IP attorneys and tech innovators who saw this gap and decided it was time to change the narrative. We believe that when women in IP have access to the right networks, mentorship, and resources, they don't just succeed — they **lead**.

## 🚀 What Does the WIPA Platform Offer?

### 1. 🏠 Social Feed & Community Hub
A dynamic, real-time social feed where members can share insights, celebrate wins, post industry news, and engage with their peers through likes, comments, and rich media posts. Think of it as LinkedIn, but built specifically for the IP community — curated, focused, and free from noise.

### 2. 💬 Direct Messaging (Real-time)
Have a private conversation with anyone on the platform. Our messaging system is **fully real-time** — messages appear instantly, unread badges update live across the sidebar and conversation list, and you can initiate a chat directly from any member's profile page. No delays, no refreshes.

### 3. 👥 Members Directory & Smart Search
Browse through a comprehensive directory of WIPA members, searchable by name, role, company, and specialty. Powered by advanced search, you can find the exact person you're looking for in seconds — whether it's a patent counsel in Tokyo or a trademark specialist in New York.

### 4. 🤝 Connections & Networking
Send connection requests, accept invitations, and build your professional circle. Connection statuses update **in real-time** across the entire platform — from individual profile pages to the global Members Directory. Your network grows with you.

### 5. 🔔 Real-time Notifications
Never miss a beat. Our notification system delivers instant alerts for connection requests, acceptances, messages, and platform activity. Each notification links directly to the relevant profile or conversation, and you can mark all as read or delete individually.

### 6. 🧑‍💼 Professional Profiles & Digital Business Cards
Your WIPA profile is more than a bio — it's a **professional portfolio**. Showcase your:
- **Company & Role** — Let people know where you work and what you do.
- **Years of Experience** — Display your seniority at a glance.
- **Education** — Highlight your academic background.
- **Skills** — Tag your areas of expertise for discoverability.
- **Certifications & Awards** — Show off your credentials and achievements.
- **Featured Projects** — Showcase case studies, publications, or landmark cases.

Every profile comes with a **shareable QR code** (with the WIPA logo embedded in the center) that you can download in HD and share at conferences, on business cards, or on social media. One scan takes anyone directly to your public digital business card.

### 7. 🎓 Mentorship Program *(NEW)*
Our dedicated Mentorship hub connects experienced IP professionals with emerging talent. Whether you're looking for guidance on your first patent filing or want to give back by mentoring the next generation — WIPA makes it seamless.

### 8. 💼 Job Board
Discover career opportunities posted specifically for IP professionals. From associate positions at top law firms to in-house IP counsel roles at Fortune 500 companies, the WIPA Job Board is your go-to destination.

### 9. 📅 Events & Conferences
Stay connected with the global IP community through curated events — from virtual webinars and panel discussions to in-person conferences and networking mixers. Each event has its own dedicated page with full details, speaker bios, and registration.

### 10. 💬 Forums & Discussion Boards
Dive deep into IP topics with structured, threaded discussions. Whether it's a debate about AI-generated inventions or best practices for international trademark filings, the Forums are where knowledge is shared and ideas are born.

### 11. 👥 Groups
Join or create interest-based groups — from "Women in Biotech Patents" to "IP Law Students Network." Groups give you a focused space to collaborate with like-minded professionals on the topics that matter most to you.

### 12. 📚 Resources & Learning
Access curated resources, courses, and educational materials designed to help you grow. From patent drafting fundamentals to advanced IP strategy, our resource library is built to support professionals at every stage of their career.

### 13. 💳 Membership Tiers (Stripe-powered)
WIPA offers flexible, tier-based membership plans to suit every professional:
- **Student** — For law students and early-career professionals breaking into IP.
- **IP Professional** — For practicing attorneys, examiners, and in-house counsel.
- **Startup** — For founders and innovation teams building IP portfolios.

All payments are securely processed through **Stripe** with automated subscription management, webhook-driven tier upgrades, and seamless onboarding.

## 🏗️ How is WIPA Built?

WIPA is built on a modern, production-grade tech stack designed for performance, scalability, and real-time interactivity:

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| **Frontend** | Next.js (React), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS |
| **Backend / Database** | Supabase (PostgreSQL), Row Level Security (RLS) |
| **Real-time** | Supabase Realtime (WebSockets for messaging, notifications, presence) |
| **Authentication** | Supabase Auth (email/password, OAuth-ready) |
| **Payments** | Stripe Checkout, Webhooks, Subscription Management |
| **Search** | Upstash (fast, edge-native search) |
| **Media Storage** | Supabase Storage (avatars, cover photos, uploads) |
| **QR Codes** | `qrcode` library with custom logo embedding & HD canvas rendering |
| **Hosting** | Vercel (Edge Functions, CI/CD from GitHub) |
| **Animations** | Framer Motion |
